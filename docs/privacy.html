TalentProfile Autofill privacy

What the extension reads, stores and sends.

Draft. Not yet approved.

This page covers the TalentProfile Autofill Chrome extension. Your TalentProfile account is covered by the TalentProfile privacy policy.

#Where data goes

  • The extension talks only to TalentProfile servers at backend.talentprofile.net.
  • It sends no data to third parties.
  • It contains no analytics, tracking or remote error reporting.

#What the extension reads from TalentProfile

  • Your profile: name, contact details, links, work history, education, skills, languages, rates and summary.
  • Your saved application answers.
  • Your notes.
  • Your CV file, only when a form has a file field to fill.

#What the extension sends to TalentProfile

  • Application answers. When you fill or edit fields on a job application, the extension saves the question and your answer to your TalentProfile account. This happens when you submit the application, when you leave the page after a submit attempt, and as a draft shortly after you change a field. Each saved answer includes the question text, field type, your answer, the page address and the application platform.
  • Notes. Notes you add, edit or delete in the picker.
  • Sign-in tokens. Used to authenticate each request and to refresh your session.

File fields are never captured. Workday password fields are never captured.

#What stays in your browser

  • Sign-in tokens stay in extension storage until you sign out.
  • The list of sites you enabled and the suggestion setting stay until you change them.
  • A copy of your profile is cached for up to 5 minutes in session storage. Chrome clears it when the browser closes.
  • Answers waiting to upload are kept in session storage for at most 30 minutes.
  • Your CV is held in memory only.

#On-device suggestions

  • The suggestion model ships inside the extension and runs in your browser.
  • Model input and results are not sent anywhere.
  • Suggestions are on by default. Turn them off with Classifier suggestions (beta) in the popup settings.

#Permissions

PermissionWhy
storageKeep sign-in, enabled sites, settings and the profile cache.
tabsFind the application tab, open sign-in and dashboard pages.
webNavigationFind application forms inside frames, and notice when you leave a page.
scriptingRun the extension on sites you enable.
offscreenRun the on-device suggestion model.
Workday and Greenhouse hostsWork on supported job sites right after install.
talentprofile.net hostsSign in and load your profile.
Other sites (optional)Asked one site at a time, only when you click Enable here. Removed when you disable the site.

#Sign out

  • Sign out in the popup settings removes your tokens, the profile cache and the cached CV from the browser.
  • Answers and notes already saved to your account stay in your account.

#Contact

Questions about your data: talentprofile.net/contact.
