Supported and enabled sites

Some job sites work right after install. Any other site works after you enable it.

#Supported sites

PlatformHosts
Workday*.myworkdayjobs.com
Greenhouseboards.greenhouse.io
Greenhouse (new job boards)job-boards.greenhouse.io

#Enable another site

  1. Open the application page.
  2. Open the popup. It shows Not enabled on and the site's host.
  3. Click Enable here.
  4. Chrome asks for access to that site. Click Allow.

The extension gets access to that one site only. If you decline, the popup shows Permission denied.

#Embedded forms

Some career pages load the application form from another site. The popup lists them under Embedded forms on this page:. Click Enable or Disable next to each form.

#Disable a site

  1. Open the popup and click the gear icon.
  2. Click Manage enabled sites.
  3. Click ✕ next to the site.
  4. Confirm with Disable.

Disabling removes the extension's access to that site.
