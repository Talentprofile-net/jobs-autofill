Picker and notes

The picker inserts one value from your profile, or one of your notes, into the focused field.

#Open the picker

  1. Assign a key to Open TalentProfile picker on focused field. See Shortcuts.
  2. Click into a field on the application.
  3. Press your picker key.

The picker command has no default key. Assign one first.

#Menu

GroupValues
IdentityFirst name, Middle name, Last name, Full name, Email, Phone
LinksLinkedIn URL, GitHub URL, Personal website
WorkCurrent title, Years of experience
LocationLocation
RatesHourly rate, Monthly rate, Currency
SummaryYour profile summary
NotesYour notes, and Add note

Empty values are hidden. A group with only one value shows that value directly.

#Insert a value

Click a value. The picker inserts it into the field and closes.

#Notes

Notes are reusable text snippets, such as a short cover note. They are saved to your TalentProfile account.

Add a note

  1. Open the picker and go to Notes.
  2. Click Add note.
  3. Write the note. The limit is 4000 characters.
  4. Click Save, or press ⌘/Ctrl+Enter.

Edit or delete a note

In Notes, use Edit note or Delete note on the note. Deleting asks Delete this note? first.

#Suggested answer

With classifier suggestions turned on, the picker can show one of your saved answers marked Suggested answer.
