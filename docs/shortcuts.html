Reference

Keyboard shortcuts

One shortcut has a default key. Assign keys to the other two yourself.

CommandDefault key
Open TalentProfileCtrl+Shift+L
macOS: ⌘+Shift+L
Open TalentProfile picker on focused fieldNone
Fill all visible fields with TalentProfileNone

#Assign a key

  1. Open the popup, click the gear icon, then click Keyboard shortcuts. Or open chrome://extensions/shortcuts.
  2. Find TalentProfile Autofill.
  3. Click the edit button next to the command and press the keys you want.
